Mathematics textbooks written from the ground up, with the problem sets and
figures to go with them.

Every book here starts as notes taken while working through the subject, then
gets rewritten into something worth reading on its own. Every figure is drawn
with [the Maximum Mathematics Asymptote library]({{% relref "/asymptote" %}}),
which is documented here too — it is free to use, whether you are reading these
books or writing your own.
