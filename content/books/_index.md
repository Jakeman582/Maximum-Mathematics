Each book is a section of this site: chapters read in order, with problem sets
at the end of each one.

Problem sets come in three tiers:

| Tier | Who it is for | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| **Practice problems** | Everyone | Free, with worked solutions |
| **Problem sets** | Readers who want more | Free problems; solutions sold separately |
| **Instructor sets** | Verified instructors | Free, including full solutions |

Accounts, purchasing, and instructor verification are not built yet. Until they
are, everything published here is free.
