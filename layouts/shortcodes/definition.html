{{- /* definition — introduces one or more terms. Call with the angle-bracket delimiter form (see notice-box.html for why). Parameters: terms — comma-separated list of the word(s) or phrase(s) being defined, in natural case; the title bar renders them capitalized The title bar shows only the term(s) themselves — no surrounding sentence — so the box reads like a dictionary entry.
